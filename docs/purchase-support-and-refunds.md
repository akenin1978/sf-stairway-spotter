# Explorer purchase support and refunds

## What support should ask first

- Which Apple Account made the purchase?
- Does the App Store purchase history show the purchase?
- Is the device signed into the same Apple Account?
- Has the customer selected Restore Purchases?
- What app version, iOS version, and device are they using?

Do not request Apple Account passwords, authentication codes, or full payment information.

## Restore Purchases

Lifetime Explorer access should be restored from the current App Store entitlement after reinstalling the app or moving to another device. Restoring must not charge the customer again.

If restoration fails, ask the customer to confirm network access and the Apple Account used for the original purchase, restart the app, and try Restore Purchases again. Escalate persistent failures with the app version, device model, iOS version, approximate purchase date, and Apple order ID if the customer chooses to provide it.

## Refunds

Apple processes App Store refund requests. Direct customers to Apple’s Report a Problem service at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/. Do not promise that a refund will be approved or provide a specific processing time.

A refunded or revoked purchase should remove the Explorer entitlement after the app refreshes its App Store purchase status. Existing private history should remain, but the customer should no longer be able to use paid-only capabilities unless they purchase again.

Deleting an SF Stairway Spotter account does not itself request or guarantee an App Store refund. Explain this before deletion wherever purchase-linked accounts are supported.

## Public support response

“If you previously purchased Explorer, open the purchase screen or Settings and select Restore Purchases. Restoring does not charge you again. For refund requests, use Apple’s Report a Problem service. If restoration still fails, contact Support with your device model, iOS version, app version, and approximate purchase date. Never send your Apple Account password or authentication code.”
