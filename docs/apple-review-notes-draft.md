# Apple App Review notes — draft

SF Stairway Spotter lets people browse San Francisco public stairways without creating an account. Account features include a private Spotted checklist, on-site verified visits, badges, statistics, optional leaderboard participation, mayorships, and Friends.

## Review access

Use the demo account credentials supplied in App Store Connect. The account is configured with representative progress and social features. Please keep the production backend available during review.

## Verification

An on-site verification requires the reviewer to be physically near a mapped stairway and to allow location and camera access. The camera image is checked temporarily on the device and is not saved or uploaded. Precise verification location is not stored with the visit.

If physical verification is impractical during review, the submitted demo video shows the complete on-site flow. No external hardware is required.

## Explorer purchase

The first 10 unique verified stairways are free. The Founding Explorer Lifetime in-app purchase unlocks unlimited verification, continued badge and leaderboard progress, mayorship competition, repeat-visit tracking, and future premium exploration tools. The app displays the localized StoreKit price. A Restore Purchases control is available from the purchase screen and Settings.

## Safety and privacy

Before the first verification, each account must acknowledge the in-app outdoor-safety message. Users can opt out of the leaderboard, remove or block friends, report users, and permanently delete their account in Settings. Support, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and account-deletion information are linked in the app.
