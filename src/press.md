### 1. What is SF Stairway Spotter?

SF Stairway Spotter is a map and exploration app devoted to San Francisco’s public stairways. Anyone can browse photographs and stairway details without an account. With a free account, people can maintain a private checklist of spotted stairways and connect with friends. Verified visits contribute to progress toward badges and optional community features, including a leaderboard and stairway mayorships.

### 2. What inspired the project?

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Alexandra Kenin set out to visit and photograph San Francisco’s public stairways. She began by digitizing the stairway index from Stairway Walks in San Francisco, then expanded the collection through her own explorations.

She initially shared her discoveries through a [Google My Map](https://bit.ly/sfstairmap). After the map received more than 260,000 views, she saw an opportunity to turn it into something more mobile-friendly and interactive: an app that would help others discover the stairways and track their own explorations.

### 3. Who created SF Stairway Spotter?

Alexandra Kenin is an urban hiking guide, author, and founder of Urban Hiker SF. Originally from Wyckoff, New Jersey, she has called San Francisco home since 2007.

After leaving a job at Google in 2012, Alexandra began exploring the city on foot and turned that passion into her urban hiking tour company. She and her team have since guided thousands of people from dozens of countries.

Her hiking guidebooks—[Urban Trails: San Francisco](https://bookshop.org/a/80384/9781680510201), [Urban Trails: East Bay](https://bookshop.org/a/80384/9781680512564), and [Urban Trails: Peninsula & South Bay](https://bookshop.org/a/80384/9781680517309)—have sold more than 30,000 copies combined.

You can follow her urban adventures on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/urbanhikersf) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/urbanhikersf).

### 4. How many stairways does the app document?

The collection includes more than 1,250 public stairways across San Francisco’s neighborhoods, parks, and islands, each photographed firsthand. Alexandra began the project in 2020 and continues to expand it as she discovers additional stairways.

### 5. What makes San Francisco’s public stairways distinctive?

San Francisco’s public stairways turn steep hills into neighborhood connections, unexpected shortcuts, and places to pause for a view.

Some are historic, offering glimpses of how people navigated the city’s hills as neighborhoods developed. Others are simple and functional, connecting streets, homes, parks, and trails. Still others are works of art, with colorful painted risers, intricate mosaics, and gardens that make the stairway itself as beautiful as the view from the top.

From a modest flight of concrete steps to a celebrated mosaic stairway, each offers a different perspective on San Francisco—and a reason to explore beyond your usual route.

### 6. How is the app different from an ordinary map or walking app?

Rather than treating stairways as incidental parts of a route, SF Stairway Spotter makes them the destination. It combines firsthand photography and stairway-specific details with a personal checklist, verified visits, badges, and optional social features.

The collection reflects years of Alexandra’s on-foot exploration. It goes beyond well-known mosaic steps to include overlooked neighborhood shortcuts, park stairways, and modest flights of steps that might otherwise escape notice.

### 7. How does visit verification work?

When someone is physically near a mapped stairway, they can select “Verify today’s visit.” The app checks their location and uses a temporary camera photo as an additional on-site action. The photo is not saved or uploaded.

Verified visits contribute to progress, badges, and optional community features. The leaderboard counts unique verified stairways, while stairway mayorships recognize repeat visits during a rolling 30-day period.

Individual visit dates, temporary verification photos, and precise visit locations are not shown publicly.

### 8. When and where is the app launching?

SF Stairway Spotter is targeting an October 2026 launch, pending App Store approval, to coincide with San Francisco’s Stairway Month.

The app will launch first on iOS, with Android planned next. Its stairway collection focuses on San Francisco, but anyone can explore the web map at [sfstairwayspotter.app](https://www.sfstairwayspotter.app).

### 9. Is the app free?

Anyone can browse the map, photographs, and stairway details for free without an account. With a free account, users can maintain an unlimited private checklist of spotted stairways and connect with friends. Their first 10 unique verified stairways are also free, allowing them to try verification and earn introductory badges.

When users pay for the app, they unlock unlimited verified visits, repeat-visit tracking, the full badge collection, mayorships, and the leaderboard.

Planned pricing is a one-time payment of $6.99 during Stairway Month and $9.99 afterward, with either purchase providing lifetime access to paid features.
