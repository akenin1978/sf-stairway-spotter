### About the app

Anyone can browse the map, photographs, and stairway details without an account. With a free account, people can keep a private checklist of spotted stairways and connect with friends.

While physically at a stairway, users can verify their visit to earn progress toward badges and participate in optional community features. The leaderboard counts unique verified stairways; stairway mayorships recognize repeat visits during a rolling 30-day period.

The app makes the stairways themselves a destination—whether someone is discovering a new neighborhood or taking a different route through a familiar one.

### From a pandemic project to an app

In 2020, urban hiking guide and author Alexandra Kenin set out to visit and photograph San Francisco’s public stairways. She began by digitizing the stairway index from *Stairway Walks in San Francisco*, then expanded the collection through her own explorations.

Alexandra shared her discoveries through a [Google My Map](https://bit.ly/sfstairmap), which has received more than 260,000 views. That interest inspired a more mobile-friendly, interactive experience: an app that helps people find stairways and track their own adventures.

SF Stairway Spotter reflects six years of on-foot exploration, with the collection continuing to grow as Alexandra discovers additional stairways. She used Codex by OpenAI to help turn that fieldwork into an app, with little prior coding experience.

### About Alexandra Kenin

Alexandra Kenin is an urban hiking guide, author, and founder of [Urban Hiker SF](https://www.urbanhikersf.com). Originally from Wyckoff, New Jersey, she has called San Francisco home since 2007.

After leaving Google in 2012, Alexandra turned her love of exploring the city on foot into an urban hiking tour company. She and her team have since guided thousands of people from dozens of countries.

She is the author of three Bay Area hiking guidebooks: [*Urban Trails: San Francisco*](https://bookshop.org/a/80384/9781680510201), [*Urban Trails: East Bay*](https://bookshop.org/a/80384/9781680512564), and [*Urban Trails: Peninsula & South Bay*](https://bookshop.org/a/80384/9781680517309).

Follow her urban adventures on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/urbanhikersf) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/urbanhikersf).

### Planned launch pricing

The planned free offering includes map browsing, stairway photographs and details, an unlimited private spotted checklist, friend connections, and the first 10 unique verified stairways.

A paid upgrade is planned to unlock unlimited verified visits, repeat-visit tracking, the full badge collection, mayorships, and leaderboard participation.

Planned pricing is a **one-time payment of $6.99 during Stairway Month and $9.99 afterward**. These plans describe the intended public launch offering; paid limits are not currently enforced in the beta.
